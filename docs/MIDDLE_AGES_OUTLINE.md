# Middle Ages Track: 30-Lesson Outline
## Medieval Christianity A.D. 590–1073

### **PART I: FOUNDATIONS (Lessons 1–6)**

1. **What Was the Medieval Church?**
   - Definition, time period, major themes
   - The fall of Rome and rise of the Middle Ages
   - Key: geographic fragmentation, spiritual authority

2. **The World of Medieval Christianity**
   - Barbarian kingdoms of Western Europe
   - The Kelts, Teutons, and Slavs
   - Trade routes and communication

3. **Gregory the Great (A.D. 590–604)**
   - Pope Gregory I: founder of medieval papacy
   - Missions to convert barbarians
   - Liturgy, theology, and influence on medieval church

4. **The Genius of Medieval Christianity**
   - Monastic tradition and its power
   - Fusion of Germanic and Christian cultures
   - Feudal structures and the church

5. **Early Medieval Missions: The Great Movements**
   - Strategy of missionary work
   - Role of monasteries as missionary centers
   - The conversion of entire peoples

6. **Timeline and Periods of the Middle Ages**
   - Early Middle Ages (590–800)
   - High Middle Ages (800–1073)
   - Key turning points and dynasties

---

### **PART II: THE BRITISH ISLES & CELTIC MISSIONS (Lessons 7–12)**

7. **The Conversion of Ireland: St. Patrick**
   - Patrick's life and mission (5th century)
   - How Ireland became Christian
   - The Irish Church's unique character

8. **The Irish Monastery Movement**
   - Monasteries as centers of learning and spirituality
   - The "Age of Saints" in Ireland
   - Irish monks and missionaries

9. **St. Columba and Iona**
   - Founder of Iona monastery
   - Mission to Scotland
   - Influence on Northern Europe

10. **The Conversion of Anglo-Saxon England**
    - Augustine's mission (A.D. 595)
    - The Synod of Whitby (664)
    - Roman vs. Celtic practices

11. **The Culdees and Celtic Christianity**
    - Unique practices of the Celtic Church
    - Merger with Roman Christianity
    - Preservation of learning

12. **The Conversion of Scotland**
    - St. Ninian and early missions
    - Role of Iona
    - Final triumph of Rome under King David I

---

### **PART III: THE FRANKISH KINGDOM & CHARLEMAGNE (Lessons 13–17)**

13. **Clovis and the Conversion of the Franks**
    - Why Clovis chose Christianity (A.D. 496)
    - The Frankish kingdom becomes Christian
    - Alliance of church and state

14. **Irish Missionaries on the Continent**
    - Columbanus and his followers
    - Monasteries in Gaul
    - Spread of Celtic monastic tradition

15. **Boniface: The Apostle of Germany (A.D. 680–754)**
    - English missionary to German tribes
    - Missions and martyrdom
    - Founding of monasteries and dioceses

16. **Charlemagne (A.D. 768–814) and the Church**
    - The Emperor and the Pope
    - Charlemagne's reform of the church
    - The Carolingian Renaissance

17. **The Holy Roman Empire (A.D. 800)**
    - Crowning of Charlemagne as Emperor
    - Church-state relationship redefined
    - Legacy and influence

---

### **PART IV: NORTHERN & EASTERN MISSIONS (Lessons 18–22)**

18. **Scandinavian Heathenism and the North**
    - Norse religion and culture
    - Viking raids and the Christian response
    - Why conversion was difficult

19. **St. Ansgar: Apostle of the North (A.D. 801–865)**
    - Missionary to Denmark and Sweden
    - The Christianization of Scandinavia
    - Challenges and setbacks

20. **The Christianization of Scandinavian Lands**
    - Denmark's conversion (10th century)
    - Sweden and Norway
    - Iceland and the sagas

21. **Cyrillus and Methodius: Apostles of the Slavs**
    - Mission to Moravia and the Balkans
    - The Glagolitic alphabet
    - Christianization of Eastern Europe

22. **Russia's Conversion to Christianity**
    - Vladimir the Great and Kiev
    - Connection to Byzantine Christianity
    - The Orthodox Church in the East

---

### **PART V: PAPACY & EMPIRE (Lessons 23–26)**

23. **The Rise of Papal Power (590–858)**
    - Popes after Gregory
    - Growing temporal authority
    - The Donation of Pepin and Papal States

24. **Pope Nicolas I and the Peak of Medieval Papal Power**
    - Nicolas' conflict with the Patriarch
    - Authority over emperors and bishops
    - The Pseudo-Isidorian Decretals

25. **The Degradation of the Papacy (10th Century)**
    - Political chaos in Rome
    - Corruption and decline
    - Intervention by Otto the Great

26. **The East-West Schism (1054)**
    - Photius and the first conflict
    - Cerularius and final separation
    - Greek vs. Latin Christianity

---

### **PART VI: CHURCH LIFE & PRACTICE (Lessons 27–30)**

27. **Monasticism: The Heart of Medieval Christianity**
    - St. Benedict and the Rule
    - Cluny and monastic reform
    - Monks as preservers of learning

28. **Medieval Worship, Sacraments & Saints**
    - The Mass and liturgy
    - The seven sacraments
    - Veneration of saints and images
    - The Iconoclastic controversy

29. **Doctrinal Battles of the Medieval Church**
    - The Filioque Controversy
    - Eucharistic theology
    - Predestination debates
    - Heresies (Paulicians, Euchites, Manichaeans)

30. **Learning, Literature & Culture**
    - Monasteries as schools
    - Charlemagne's educational revival
    - Figures like Bede, Alcuin, Erigena
    - Preservation of classical texts

## Design Notes

- **Progression**: Foundational concepts → Regional missions → Political/theological developments → Church life & culture
- **Balance**: Mix of biography, geography, theology, and social history
- **Depth**: Each lesson can expand into learn exercises, quizzes, and essay content
- **Primary source potential**: Schaff provides specific quotes, figures, and anecdotes for each topic
